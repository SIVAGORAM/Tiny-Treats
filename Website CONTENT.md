## 40. Important Content Rules for Development

This is the part strongly recommended to hand to the AI coding agent along with the content.

### Do NOT invent

- ❌ Product prices
- ❌ Ingredients
- ❌ Product weights
- ❌ Delivery charges
- ❌ Delivery locations
- ❌ Phone number
- ❌ WhatsApp number
- ❌ Instagram URL
- ❌ Customer testimonials
- ❌ Customer names
- ❌ Certifications
- ❌ Nutritionist approval
- ❌ Medical benefits
- ❌ Nutritional percentages
- ❌ Shelf life
- ❌ Storage instructions

...until the client supplies them.

### Be careful with these claims

The supplied documents contain stronger statements such as "perfect for growing children and expectant mothers," "nutritionist-friendly," "protein-packed," and specific ingredient/nutrition descriptions.

For the production website, keep the wording product-focused and non-medical unless Swetha provides verified ingredient/nutrition information.

| Instead of | Use |
|---|---|
| "Perfect for pregnant women." | "Created with families in mind." |
| "Rich in calcium and iron." | "Made with ragi and other carefully selected ingredients." |
| "Nutritionist-Friendly." | "Thoughtfully prepared snack options." |

This keeps the website premium and trustworthy without making claims the provided content doesn't substantiate.

## 📌 Why these are relatively simple for Tiny Treats

The site's actual data footprint (per the [Technical README](./README.md)) is small:

- No user accounts or logins
- No online payment processing (orders are placed via WhatsApp)
- Cart contents are stored only in the visitor's own browser (`localStorage`) — never sent to or stored on a server
- The only real "data collection" is whatever the customer voluntarily sends over WhatsApp, plus standard website analytics (if Google Analytics is installed) and any optional contact form submissions

That's worth stating plainly in the policy rather than copying a generic ecommerce template that promises things (like secure payment processing or account data handling) the site doesn't actually do.

- Understand and improve how the Site is used

3. Third-Party Services

We use WhatsApp to receive and confirm orders. Your use of WhatsApp is
also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy and terms, which we do not
control.

4. Data Retention

We retain order-related conversations (e.g. WhatsApp chat history) only
as long as reasonably necessary for order fulfilment, customer service,
and our own record-keeping.

5. Your Rights

by contacting us at [contact email/WhatsApp number]. We will respond
within a reasonable time.

6. Children's Privacy

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date
above reflects the most recent revision.

This Cookie Policy explains how www.tinytreats.in ("the Site") uses
cookies and similar technologies.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They
help websites function and, in some cases, understand how visitors use
the site.

2. What We Use

- Essential local storage: Your shopping cart is stored using your
  browser's local storage (not a traditional cookie), so your cart

  never sent to our servers.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any
time. Note that clearing your browser's local storage will also clear
your saved cart.

above reflects the most recent revision.
